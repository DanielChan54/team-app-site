10 Secret Tips For Managing A Remote Team

Alex Brown
|
July 27, 2026

Managing remote teams is challenging, but the right systems keep everyone connected and aligned.

Here are 10 practical tips to help your remote team thrive.

1. Focus on Results, Not Hours

Measure success by completed work and outcomes, not how long employees stay online. Trust your team to manage their own schedules.

2. Set Clear Expectations

Define communication channels, deadlines, responsibilities, and response times upfront. Clear expectations reduce confusion and improve accountability.

3. Keep Information in One Place

Store documents, processes, and project updates in a central hub so everyone can quickly find what they need.

4. Protect Focus Time

Limit unnecessary meetings and encourage uninterrupted blocks of deep work. Fewer distractions lead to better productivity.

5. Build Team Relationships

Remote work can feel isolating. Schedule casual check-ins or virtual social events to help strengthen team connections.

6. Embrace Asynchronous Communication

Not every message needs an immediate response. Using asynchronous communication helps employees stay focused while supporting different time zones.

7. Celebrate Successes

Recognize accomplishments, whether it's completing a project or reaching a milestone. Small moments of appreciation help boost morale.

8. Document Your Processes

Create clear documentation for workflows and procedures. It simplifies onboarding and helps your team work more independently.

9. Watch for Burnout

Regularly check in with employees about their workload and well-being. Encouraging breaks and healthy work-life balance leads to better long-term performance.

10. Empower Your Team

Avoid micromanaging. Give employees ownership over their work and trust them to make decisions within their responsibilities.

Final Thoughts

Successful remote teams are built on trust, clear communication, and strong systems. By focusing on results, supporting collaboration, and giving employees the tools they need to succeed, you can build a productive and engaged remote workforce.
